Artists & labels

Static Studios is a TikTok music marketing agency for artists and labels, working worldwide. We hand-pick creators to integrate the audio naturally into their videos, and brief them based on the track and its goals. Harvey Hilton's campaign did 10M views across 17 placements.

A campaign combines our immediate creator network with external creators posting around the sound. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches, so the set looks different for every release. Audiences can be at home or in a market the artist has not reached yet.

A campaign tests the track across several niches first, then puts more behind the ones that work, with a report per video. It can run right after a release or bring back an older track. See how a TikTok music campaign runs.

Read the full campaign: Harvey Hilton, 10M views across 17 placements. Or see what two posts on our own pages did: KayAitchBee, 2.2M+ views from 2 videos.

Whether you're the artist, the manager or the person running marketing at the label, send us the artist, the track, the socials and what you want the campaign to do, plus the release date if there is one. We'll come back on whether it's a fit, and whether a full campaign or a smaller test makes more sense to start with.
