Privacy notice

This notice explains how Static Studios processes personal data, who else is involved in running this website, the legal bases relied on, and the rights available to you. It applies to this website and to correspondence with us. It is written to be read rather than to be agreed to, and it describes what actually happens.

1. Controller

Alex Leppänen, trading as Static Studios, of Espoo, Finland, is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. Static Studios is a trading name and not a separate legal entity, so the controller is the individual named above. All enquiries relating to data protection should be sent to contact@staticstudios.io, which is monitored directly.

2. What we collect through this website

We collect no personal data through this website. It carries no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising script and no cookies. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The enquiry form does not transmit anything. It assembles a message in your own mail application, which you then choose to send or not send. Until you send it, nothing has left your device and we have no knowledge of it.

Your browser retains one short-lived technical value while you move between pages, used solely to continue a page transition across the load. It contains a timestamp and a numeric position, no personal data, and it is discarded when the tab closes.

3. What the infrastructure collects

Collecting nothing ourselves is not the same as nothing being collected, so this section sets out what happens at the level below us.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed in to GitHub. We do not have access to those logs, we cannot query them, and we do not receive any report derived from them. Their retention is determined by GitHub and governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Typefaces are requested from Google Fonts, at fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Your browser makes those requests directly, which means Google receives your IP address and the technical details that accompany any web request.

4. Information you send to us

When you write to us we receive whatever the message contains. In practice this is normally a name, an email address, the name of a project, and the details of what you are asking about. We do not require any particular information in order to reply, and we ask that you do not send us special categories of personal data.

Email is operated for us by Zoho Corporation, using Zoho Mail. Our account is registered on Zoho's European domain, which means correspondence is stored in Zoho's European Union data centre rather than outside the EEA. Zoho acts on our instructions in handling it.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Correspondence is processed in order to answer it and to carry a conversation forward. Where a working relationship follows, personal data is processed in order to perform that engagement and to meet the record-keeping obligations that come with it.

The legal bases relied on under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation are as follows. Where you approach us about possible work, the basis is the taking of steps at your request prior to entering into a contract. Where an engagement is agreed, it is the performance of that contract, and where records must be kept afterwards, it is compliance with a legal obligation. For correspondence that is not about a possible engagement, and for keeping the website available and secure, the basis is legitimate interests. We do not rely on consent, because nothing on this website requires it.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Correspondence does not leave the European Union in the ordinary course. It is held in Zoho's European Union data centre. Zoho states that support staff outside the EEA may need to access that data centre, that such access is covered by a data processing agreement based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and that there is no physical transfer of the data out of the European Union.

Two transfers do occur, neither of which involves anything you have sent us. GitHub, which hosts this website, is based in the United States and logs visitor IP addresses there. Google receives a request from your browser when the typefaces load. Both are caused by visiting the site rather than by contacting us, and both rely on the safeguards those providers have put in place. Those safeguards are the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where the provider is certified under it, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognised in July 2023 as providing an adequate level of protection.

8. Retention

Correspondence is retained for as long as it continues to serve the purpose for which it was received, and no longer. An enquiry is kept while there remains a realistic prospect of the conversation resuming. Release and launch schedules often sit a year or more out, so a thread going quiet is not by itself the end of that prospect. An engagement is kept for the duration of the work and afterwards for as long as accounting and limitation rules require.

Once none of those applies, there is no longer a basis for holding the material and it is deleted. Correspondence whose contact details and content have ceased to be relevant to any live or prospective work falls into that category.

You may ask us to delete your correspondence at any time. We will act on the request when it arrives, except where a record must be kept to meet a legal or accounting obligation, in which case we will tell you what is being retained and why.

Server logs created by GitHub are retained by GitHub under its own policy and are outside our control.

9. Campaign reporting

Campaign reporting uses the figures the platforms publish or provide about the posts we ran: views, shares, saves, engagement and comments. We do not collect data about the individuals who watched those posts, and we have no access to anything beyond public and platform-provided metrics. Where a campaign engagement is agreed, the handling of data under that engagement is governed by the agreement for it.

10. Automated decision-making

No automated decision-making or profiling producing legal or similarly significant effects is carried out.

11. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to request access to the personal data held about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased, to have processing restricted, to receive data in a portable form, and to object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests. These rights are exercised by writing to the address in section 1. We will respond within one month.

12. Complaints

If you consider that your data has been handled improperly, you are entitled to raise it with us and to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman, Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto, at tietosuoja.fi.

13. Changes to this notice

This notice describes a website that collects nothing itself and a practice that processes very little. If either changes, this notice changes with it, and the date below is updated.

Contact

Questions about this notice, requests to exercise any of the rights in section 11, and deletion requests all go to the same address.
