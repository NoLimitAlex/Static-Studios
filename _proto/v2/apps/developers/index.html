App developers

We run TikTok campaigns for apps. We hand-pick creators whose audience matches the people the app is for, brief them on what it does and what the video needs to show, and have them use it on camera. Campaigns are timed to a moment: a launch, a big feature, or a push into a new market.

Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release, and the method is the same for an app: pick the right creators, seed across a few niches, read what lands, scale what works. We work with over 60 creators, and the set is different for every launch.

We have not put an app campaign on the log yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. If you want to be the first, we will treat it that way.

Tell us the app, the platform, the date and what you want the campaign to do. We'll come back on whether creator content is the right move yet, and what it would look like.
