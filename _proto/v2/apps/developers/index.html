App developers and founders

Static Studios is a TikTok creator marketing agency for apps. We hand-pick creators whose audience matches the people the app is for, brief them on what it does and what the video needs to show, and have them work it naturally into the videos they already make. A campaign can run around a launch, a big feature or a push into a new market, or at any time: an app that is already live is the easiest to measure.

Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release, and the method is the same for an app: pick the right creators, seed across a few niches, read what lands, scale what works. We work with over 60 creators, and the set is different for every app.

We have not put an app campaign on the log yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. If you want to be the first, we will treat it that way.

Tell us the app, the platform and what you want the campaign to do, plus a date if there is one. We'll come back on whether creator content is the right move yet, and what it would look like.
