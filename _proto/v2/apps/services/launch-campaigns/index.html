Apps / Services

Launch campaigns

Creators whose audience matches the people the app is for work it into the videos they already make. We brief each one on what the app does and what the video needs to show. It does not have to be a demo on camera: for example, a workout app inside a gym edit.

When

Before launch, in launch week, or any time after it.

How it runs

Smaller tests first across a few creator niches, then more behind the ones that work. Every view is organic. Harvey Hilton's campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release, run the same way.

What we report

We report TikTok views, shares and engagement for every video. Installs and sign-ups come from your own store data or tracking links.

Send a brief

Tell us what you are working on and what it needs to do. We will come back on whether it is a fit, and whether a full campaign or a smaller test makes more sense to start with.
