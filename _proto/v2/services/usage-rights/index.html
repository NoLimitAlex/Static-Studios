Using the videos on your own channels

Want to use a creator's video on your own TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Steam page, website or in ads? That is arranged separately, video by video; some creators charge extra.

Does it cost extra

Sometimes. Some creators are happy for their video to be used as it is; others charge extra for usage rights. We ask them for you, and you know whether there is an extra cost before anything is agreed.

How to ask

Tell us which videos you want and where they will run, and we will check with each creator.
