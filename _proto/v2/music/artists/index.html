Artists & labels

Static Studios is a TikTok music marketing agency for artists and labels, working worldwide. We hand-pick creators to integrate the audio naturally into their videos, and brief them based on the track and its goals. Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements.

Our campaigns combine our immediate creator network with external creators posting around the sound. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches, so the set looks different for every release. Audiences can be at home or in a market the artist has not reached yet.

How a campaign runs

We seed it first. Smaller tests go out across several niches that fit the sound, with creators picked for the track and for the audience you want to reach, including the country. Then we put more behind the niches that take to the sound best: the ones where the videos get the most views and interaction. At the end you get a report per video, with views and shares for each one, not a single total. Every view is organic: we never buy views or use bots.

There is no wrong time to run one. Right after a release works best: the song is already out, so every video sends people somewhere and the results are easy to measure. A campaign can also bring back a track that had its moment, or give an older one the push it never got.

Read the full campaign: Harvey, 10M views across 17 placements. Or see what two posts on our own pages did: KayAitchBee, 2.2M+ views from 2 videos.

Whether you're the artist, the manager or the person running marketing at the label, send us the artist, the track, the socials and what you want the campaign to do, plus the release date if there is one. We'll come back on whether it's a fit, and whether a full campaign or a smaller test makes more sense to start with.
