Artists & labels

We run TikTok campaigns for artists and labels. We hand-pick creators to integrate the audio naturally into their videos, and brief them based on the track and its goals. Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements.

Our campaigns combine our immediate creator network with external creators posting around the sound. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches, so the set looks different for every release. Audiences can be at home or in a market the artist has not reached yet.

How a campaign runs

We start small. A handful of creators post with the track across a few niches, picked for the sound and for the audience you want to reach, including the country. Over the first days we watch which videos are moving, then put the rest of the campaign behind those. At the end you get a report per video, with views and shares for each one, not a single total.

Two campaigns you can read in full: Harvey, 10M views across 17 placements and KayAitchBee, 2.2M+ views from 2 videos.

Whether you're the artist, the manager or the person running marketing at the label, send us the artist, the track, the release date, the socials and what the release needs to do. We'll come back on whether it's a fit, and whether a full campaign or a smaller test makes more sense to start with.
