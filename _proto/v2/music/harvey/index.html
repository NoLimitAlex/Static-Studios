The Brief

Harvey Hilton had an unreleased track with a release date one week out. It needed exposure: real people hearing it before and as it dropped.

The Approach

We ran the track through our UGC creator network, placing it with creators whose audiences already respond to that kind of sound. Campaigns can be aimed at a specific audience or territory, or left to our team to direct.

10,007,951 views across 17 placements, with 256,894 shares and a 19,3 % engagement rate. Shares matter more than the view count here: a share is somebody putting the track in front of their own audience, which is the kind of organic discovery that keeps working after a campaign ends.
