TikTok music campaigns

The main thing we do. We pick creators for the track, brief each one on the song and what it needs to do, and they post it in their own style, so the sound sits naturally in their videos rather than reading as an ad.

How we pick the creators

By the track and by the audience you want to reach. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches, and bring in creators from outside that network when the song needs them. Each creator is picked for the kind of video they make and for where their audience is.

Test first, then scale

We seed the track first: smaller tests across several niches that fit the sound. Then we put more of the campaign behind the niches that take to it best, where the videos get the most views and interaction. A bigger budget means a longer and wider testing stage, so more is learned before we scale.

What you get

A report for every video: views, shares, engagement rate and what each thousand views cost. Not one total. Every view is organic: we never buy views or use bots, and we do not promise a number of views.

When to run one

Any time. Right after a release is easiest to measure, because every video sends people to a song that is already out. A campaign can also go out before a release, bring back a track that had its moment, or give an older one the push it never got.

How long it takes

A typical campaign runs about two to three weeks, depending on its size and goals. It can be rushed, but a shorter testing stage means less certainty about where to scale. It can be a one-off or run month to month.
