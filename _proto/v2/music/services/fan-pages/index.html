Music / Services

Artist fan pages

A TikTok page dedicated to one artist, set up and run by us every month. It keeps the artist's songs in front of people on a steady schedule, including between releases, when there is often nothing else going out.

What it posts

Edits, lyric videos and clips built from the artist's songs.

What we agree first

  • How often it posts.
  • How it looks.
  • What it can and cannot use.
  • How the page presents itself.

Replying to fans

Replying to comments, to keep the page talking with its community, can be added on top.

A fan page can run on its own or next to a creator campaign or page placements.
