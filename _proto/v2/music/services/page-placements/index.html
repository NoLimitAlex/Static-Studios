Page placements

Posts on TikTok pages we run ourselves and pages we work with. Each page is built around one kind of content: memes, emotional edits, gaming clips. Your track goes out as the sound on those posts.

Why it moves quickly

On the pages we run ourselves, the timing and style of each post are in our hands, with no creators to brief first. That makes placements a quick way to get a sound moving, on their own or next to a creator campaign.

How it works

You give us the track and the goal; we pick the pages. Every view is organic: we never buy views or use bots.

What two placements on our own pages did: KayAitchBee, 2.2M+ views from 2 videos, with one of them the most viewed and most liked video on the sound at the time.
