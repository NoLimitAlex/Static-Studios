International audience campaigns

We pick creators for where their audience is, not just what they post. If two creators make the same kind of video and one's audience is mostly in the US while the other's is at home, a campaign aimed at the US gets the first one.

Where we reach

Creators are picked by where their audience is, so a campaign can be aimed at the US, Europe, the UK or Canada.

Why it matters

A market the artist has not reached yet is usually the hardest part of a release. Picking creators by audience country is how a campaign gets there.

Every creator we work with shares their top three audience countries with us, so the pick is based on their real numbers.
