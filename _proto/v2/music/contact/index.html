Static Studios is a short-form and UGC agency. Founded in 2026 and based in Espoo, Finland, we run TikTok campaigns for artists, labels and managers internationally. Inquire about:

TikTok music campaigns

Campaign planning, creator sourcing and delivery for labels, artists and managers. One-off or ongoing. Campaigns can combine our in-house accounts with external TikTok pages. Reporting covers views, shares, engagement rate and cost per thousand views, per video.

Sound promotion & page placements

Promote a track through the TikTok accounts we work with, as standalone placements or as part of a wider campaign. Account details are available on request.

Artist fan pages

Monthly TikTok fan-page work for artists and labels. Posting volume, content direction, aesthetics and community interaction are agreed per project.

International audience campaigns

The creators we work with are not limited to one territory. Campaigns can be aimed at audiences outside an artist's home market, which is usually the hardest part of a release to reach.

Tell us the artist, the track, the date and roughly what you have set aside, and we will propose a campaign or a smaller test.
