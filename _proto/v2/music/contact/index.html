TikTok music campaigns

The main thing we do. We pick creators for the track, brief each one on the song and what it needs to do, and they post it in their own style. We seed it first: smaller tests across several niches that fit the sound. Then we put more behind the niches that take to it best, where the videos get the most views and interaction. It can be for a new release, a track that is already out, or one that had its moment and deserves another, as a one-off or month to month. You get a report for every video: views, shares, engagement rate and what each thousand views cost.

Page placements

Posts on TikTok pages we run ourselves and pages we work with, each built around one kind of content: memes, emotional edits, gaming clips. Your track goes out as the sound on those posts, on its own or next to a creator campaign. Because the timing and the style of each post are in our hands, it is the quickest way to get a sound moving. You give us the track and the goal; we pick the pages. Two placements on our own pages did 2.2M+ views for KayAitchBee.

Learn more about page placements →

Artist fan pages

A TikTok page dedicated to one artist, set up and run by us every month. It posts edits, lyric videos and clips built from the artist's songs on a steady schedule, so there is always something putting the music in front of people, including between releases. Before we start, we agree with you how often it posts, how it looks and what it can and cannot use. Replying to fans in the comments, to keep the page talking with its community, can be added on top.

Learn more about artist fan pages →

International audience campaigns

We pick creators for where their audience is, not just what they post. If two creators make the same kind of video and one's audience is mostly in the US while the other's is at home, a campaign aimed at the US gets the first one. That lets a campaign reach a market the artist has not reached yet, which is usually the hardest part of a release.

Learn more about international audience campaigns →

Using the videos on your own channels

Want to repost a creator's video on the artist's or label's TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, website or in ads? That is arranged on top of the campaign, video by video. Some creators are happy for it to be used as it is; others charge extra for usage rights. We ask them for you, and you know whether there is an extra cost before anything is agreed.
