TikTok music campaigns

The main thing we do. We pick creators for the track, brief each one on the song and what it needs to do, and they post it in their own style. We start with a few, see which videos move, and put the rest of the campaign behind those. It can be for a new release, a track that is already out, or one that had its moment and deserves another, as a one-off or month to month. You get a report for every video: views, shares, engagement rate and what each thousand views cost.

Page placements

Posts on TikTok pages we run, each built around one kind of content: memes, emotional edits, gaming clips. Your track goes out as the sound on those posts, on its own or next to a creator campaign. Because we decide when and how the posts go out, it is the quickest way to get a sound moving. Tell us the track and we will send back the pages that fit it.

Artist fan pages

A TikTok page dedicated to one artist, run by us. We post edits, lyric videos and clips built from the artist's songs on a steady schedule, so there is always something putting the music in front of new people, including between releases. A fan page reaches people the artist's own account often does not, because it reads as fan content rather than promotion. Before we start, we agree with you how often it posts, how it looks, what it can and cannot use, and how it answers fans in the comments. It runs month to month.

International audience campaigns

The creators we work with are not limited to one territory. Campaigns can be aimed at audiences outside an artist's home market, which is usually the hardest part of a release to reach.

Creators: work with us

Post on TikTok in meme, emotional, gaming or another niche? We pay creators to use a track in their own style of video, with a clear brief on the song, the mood and what the video needs to do. No exclusivity, you keep posting your own stuff. See what we ask for, or email us your handle.
