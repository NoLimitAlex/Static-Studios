Campaign 002 / Case Study

Say Shit

KayAitchBee

The Brief

KayAitchBee approached Static Studios to post two videos for “Say Shit”. A small, contained scope: two posts, run from accounts we own rather than a wide creator buy.

The Approach

The campaign was run entirely on Static Studios’ own in-house short-form accounts. Posting from accounts we manage and operate enables us to execute deliverables faster and with less setup. The campaign was deliberately small: two posts, and one of them finished as the most viewed and most liked video on the sound, ahead of every other post using the track.

The Result

2.2M+
Views

Across two videos. One of the two became the most viewed and most liked video on the sound, out of every post using the track. Two posts, placed well, outperforming the rest of the sound’s catalogue.

Owned distribution. This campaign ran entirely through short-form accounts Static Studios built and operates, not through booked third-party creators.
