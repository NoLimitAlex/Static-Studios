Work with us

We work with TikTok creators across meme, emotional, gaming and more. When a campaign fits your page, you get a paid placement with a clear brief: the sound, game or app, what the video needs to do, and the deadline. No exclusivity. You keep posting your own stuff.

To get on file, send us four things:

  1. Your median views per video over the last 28 days.
  2. Your top three audience countries.
  3. The save and share rate on a recent sponsored post.
  4. Your rate per video.

Add your handle and the niches you post in. We'll confirm when you're on file, and reach out when a campaign fits.
