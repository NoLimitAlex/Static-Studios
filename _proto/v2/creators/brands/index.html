How booking works

  1. Tell us what it is, what the videos need to do and where the audience should be.
  2. We suggest creators whose style fits, with their numbers: lifetime views, median views over the last 28 days, views over the last 60 days, top audience countries and ages, and save and share rate.
  3. Each creator sees the brief and says yes or no.
  4. We handle the rate, the brief and the posting dates.

Emailing a creator’s @staticstudios.io address?

That comes to us. We reply from the same inbox and handle the booking for the creator.
