For creators

Work with us

There are two ways in. Most creators start with campaigns, and some hand us their brand deals as well. You can do one or both.

Campaigns

When a music, game or app campaign fits your page, you get a paid placement with a clear brief: the sound, game or app, what the video needs to do, and the deadline. No exclusivity: you keep posting your own stuff.

Management

We handle your brand deals through your own name@staticstudios.io address. See how management works.

What to send

Your handle, the niches you post in, and whether you want campaigns, management or both. Plus these numbers:

  1. Your lifetime views.
  2. Your median views per video over the last 28 days.
  3. Your total views over the last 60 days.
  4. Your top three audience countries and your audience's age split.
  5. The save and share rate on a recent sponsored post.
  6. Your rate per video.

We confirm when you are on file and come back when something fits.

Niches

Meme, emotional, gaming and more. If you post in a clear style, send it.
