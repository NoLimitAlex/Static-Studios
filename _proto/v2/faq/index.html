General

What does Static Studios do?

We run TikTok creator campaigns for artists and labels, game studios and app developers. We hand-pick creators for each release, brief them on the track, game or app and its goals, and report on every video.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We are based in Espoo, Finland, and work with clients internationally. We reach audiences best in the US and Europe, and also run campaigns for the UK and Canada.

Are the views real?

Yes. Every view is organic: we never buy views or use bots.

Do you guarantee a number of views?

No. We do not promise a number of views. We test first, then put more behind what works, and you see the numbers for every video.

How do you pick the creators?

By the release and by the audience you want to reach: the kind of video each creator makes and where their audience is. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches, and bring in others when a campaign needs them.

Do we choose the creators?

We pick them. A campaign uses many creators, so you set the goals and the audience, and we do the picking.

What does a campaign cost?

It depends on the goals and the size of the campaign. The budget sets the scale: a bigger budget means longer and wider testing before we scale, so more is learned. Tell us the goal and roughly what you have set aside, and we will say what makes sense, including a smaller test.

Can we start small?

Yes. A smaller test across a few niches shows where it lands before you put more behind it.

How long does a campaign take?

A typical campaign runs about two to three weeks, depending on its size and goals. It can be rushed, but a shorter testing stage means less certainty about where to scale.

How soon can it start?

As soon as the details are agreed and payment is in. We read email every day and reply fast.

What do we get at the end?

A report for every video, not one total.

Can it run month to month?

Yes. A campaign can be a one-off or ongoing work, month to month.

Can we reuse the videos on our own channels?

That is arranged on top of the campaign, video by video. Some creators charge extra for usage rights and some do not, and you know which before anything is agreed.

Music

Does the song need a release date?

No. Right after a release is easiest to measure, but a campaign can go out before a release, bring back a track that had its moment, or push an older one.

What does a music report include?

Views, shares, engagement rate and what each thousand views cost, for every video.

Can you aim a campaign at one country?

Yes. We pick creators for where their audience is, so a campaign for the US uses creators whose audience is mostly in the US.

What is a page placement?

Your track as the sound on posts from TikTok pages we run and pages we work with, each built around one kind of content. You give us the track and the goal; we pick the pages.

Do you run fan pages?

Yes. A TikTok page dedicated to one artist, set up and run by us every month. Replying to fans in the comments can be added on top.

What have you done so far?

Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements for Harvey's Dance Until We're Dead. Separately, two posts on our own pages did 2.2M+ views for KayAitchBee's Say Shit.

Games

Which games do you work with?

Steam and PC, console and Roblox mainly, and mobile games too.

At what stage can a game campaign run?

Any stage: before or after early access, during a demo or Next Fest, around launch, around an update or season, or months after release.

Have you run a game campaign yet?

Not yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. The method is the one behind our music results.

Which creators post about games?

Gaming and meme creators, picked for the game and for where their audience is.

Do you run fan pages for games?

Yes, a TikTok page built around the game, run every month. It suits some games better than others, and we will tell you honestly if yours is one of them.

Apps

Does the app have to be shown on camera?

No. Creators work the app naturally into the videos they already make. A looksmaxxing edit can carry a face analysis app as part of the edit itself.

Have you run an app campaign yet?

Not yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. The method is the one behind our music results.

Do you run fan pages for apps?

Where it fits. For some apps it is natural; for others it would be forced, and then we will say so.

Can we use the creator videos in our ads?

That is arranged on top of the campaign, video by video. Some creators charge extra for usage rights and some do not.

Creators

How do I get on file?

Email us your handle, your niches and four numbers: your median views per video over the last 28 days, your top three audience countries, the save and share rate on a recent sponsored post, and your rate per video.

Is it paid?

Yes. When a campaign fits your page, you get a paid placement with a clear brief.

Do I have to work only with you?

No. There is no exclusivity, and you keep posting your own stuff.

Which niches do you work with?

Meme, emotional and gaming, and more. If your page fits a campaign, we reach out.
