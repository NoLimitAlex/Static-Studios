General

What does Static Studios do?

We run TikTok creator campaigns for artists and labels, game studios and app developers. We hand-pick creators for each release, brief them on the track, game or app and its goals, and report on every video.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We are based in Espoo, Finland, and work with clients internationally. Creators are picked by where their audience is. Our creators' audiences are biggest in the US and Europe, and a campaign can be aimed at almost any major country.

Are the views real?

Yes. Every view is organic: we never buy views or use bots.

Do you guarantee a number of views?

No. We do not promise a number of views. We test first, then put more behind what works, and you see the numbers for every video.

How do you pick the creators?

By the release and the audience you want to reach. We work with over 60 creators across meme, emotional and gaming niches. See how it works for music, games and apps.

Do we choose the creators?

We pick them. A campaign uses many creators, so you set the goals and the audience, and we do the picking.

What does a campaign cost?

It depends on the goals and the size of the campaign. The budget sets the scale: a bigger budget means a wider testing stage and a bigger scale-up. Tell us the goal and roughly what you have set aside, and we will say what makes sense, including a smaller test.

Can we start small?

Yes. A smaller test across a few niches shows where it lands before you put more behind it.

How long does a campaign take?

About two to three weeks, depending on its size and goals. See how a campaign runs.

How soon can it start?

As soon as the details are agreed and payment is in. We read email every day and reply fast.

What do we get at the end?

A report for every video, not one total.

Can it run month to month?

Yes. A campaign can be a one-off or ongoing work, month to month.

Can we reuse the videos on our own channels?

That is arranged separately, video by video; some creators charge extra. More on usage rights.

Music

Does the song need a release date?

No. A campaign can go out before or after a release, or push an older track. More on music campaigns.

What does a music report include?

Views, shares, engagement rate and what each thousand views cost, for every video.

Can you aim a campaign at one country?

Yes. Creators are picked by where their audience is. Our creators' audiences are biggest in the US and Europe, and a campaign can be aimed at almost any major country. More on international campaigns.

What is a page placement?

Your track as the sound on posts from TikTok pages we run and pages we work with. More on page placements.

Do you run fan pages?

Yes. A TikTok page dedicated to one artist, run by us every month. More on artist fan pages.

What have you done so far?

Harvey Hilton's campaign for Dance Until We're Dead did 10M views across 17 placements. Separately, two posts on our own pages did 2.2M+ views for KayAitchBee's Say Shit.

Games

Which games is this for?

Steam and PC, console, Roblox and mobile games.

At what stage can a game campaign run?

Any stage, from a demo or early access to launch, an update or months after release. See the demo, launch and update pages.

Which creators post about games?

Gaming and meme creators, picked for the game and for where their audience is.

Do you run fan pages for games?

Yes, where it suits the game: a TikTok page built around it, run every month. More on game fan pages.

What does a games report include?

We report TikTok views, shares and engagement for every video. Wishlists and sales come from your own Steam or store data.

Apps

Does the app have to be shown on camera?

No. Creators work the app naturally into the videos they already make, for example a workout app inside a gym edit.

What does an apps report include?

We report TikTok views, shares and engagement for every video. Installs and sign-ups come from your own store data or your own tracking links.

Do you run fan pages for apps?

Where it fits, and we will say if it does not. More on app fan pages.

Can we use the creator videos in our ads?

That is arranged separately, video by video; some creators charge extra. More on usage rights.

Creators

How do I get on file?

Email us your handle, your niches and these numbers: your lifetime views, your median views per video over the last 28 days, your total views over the last 60 days, your top three audience countries and your audience's age split, the save and share rate on a recent sponsored post, and your rate per video.

Is it paid?

Yes. When a campaign fits your page, you get a paid placement with a clear brief.

Do I have to work only with you?

For campaigns, no. There is no exclusivity, and you keep posting your own stuff.

Which niches do you work with?

Meme, emotional and gaming, and more. If your page fits a campaign, we reach out.
