We run TikTok creator campaigns for game studios and publishers, from Espoo, Finland, for games played anywhere. Open a row to see what each kind of work involves, or go straight to the brief at the bottom.

Launch campaigns

Creator videos built around the game, at launch or any time after it. A game that is already out is the easiest to measure: every video points to a store page that is live. One-off or ongoing. Reporting covers views, shares and engagement rate, per video.

Early access, demo and Next Fest pushes

A smaller set of creators to get a playable build in front of the right audience before the full launch, and read what people respond to.

Update and season campaigns

Creator content around a big update, a new season or a 1.0, aimed at players who drifted off as much as at new ones.

International audience campaigns

The creators we work with are not limited to one territory. Campaigns can be aimed at a market the game has not reached yet.

Creators: work with us
