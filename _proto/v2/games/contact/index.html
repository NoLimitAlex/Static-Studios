We run TikTok creator campaigns for game studios and publishers, from Espoo, Finland, for games played anywhere. Open a row to see what each kind of work involves, or go straight to the brief at the bottom.

Launch campaigns

Creator videos built around the game, in the weeks before launch, on the day or any time after it. A game that is already out is the easiest to measure: every video points to a store page that is live. One-off or ongoing. Reporting covers views, shares and engagement rate, per video.

Early access, demo and Next Fest pushes

Creators around a playable build, whenever it is out: before or after early access goes live, during a demo or Next Fest, or between early access and 1.0. It gets the game in front of the right players and shows what they respond to before the bigger push.

Update and season campaigns

Creator content around a big update, a new season or the 1.0, or simply months after release to bring the game back up. Aimed at players who drifted off as much as at new ones.

Game fan pages

A TikTok page built around the game, set up and run by us every month: clips, edits and moments from the game on a steady schedule, so it keeps showing up for players between updates. Before we start, we agree with you how often it posts, how it looks and what footage it can use. It suits some games better than others, and we will tell you honestly if yours is one of them.

International audience campaigns

The creators we work with are not limited to one territory. Campaigns can be aimed at a market the game has not reached yet, by picking creators whose audience is mostly in that country.

Using the videos on your own channels

Want to use a creator's video on the game's TikTok, Steam page, trailer, website or in ads? That is arranged on top of the campaign, video by video. Some creators are happy for it to be used as it is; others charge extra for usage rights. We ask them for you, and you know whether there is an extra cost before anything is agreed.

Creators: work with us

Make gaming or meme videos on TikTok? Game campaigns pay creators to play and post around a launch, a demo or a big update, in their own style, with a clear brief on the game and what the video needs to do. No exclusivity. See what we ask for, or email us your handle.
