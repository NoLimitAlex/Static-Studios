Games / Services

Launch campaigns

Creators from gaming and meme niches build videos around the game, in their own style. We brief each one on the game and what the video needs to do.

When

In the weeks before launch, on the day, or any time after it.

Platforms

Steam and PC, console, Roblox and mobile games.

How it runs

We start with a few creators across different niches, see which videos players take to, then put more behind those. Every view is organic.

We report TikTok views, shares and engagement for every video. Wishlists and sales come from your own Steam or store data.

Harvey Hilton's campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release; the method is the same for a game.
