Game studios and publishers

Static Studios is a TikTok creator marketing agency for game studios and publishers, for Steam and PC, console, Roblox and mobile games. We hand-pick creators from gaming and meme niches, brief them on the game and what it needs to do, and have them build videos around it. A campaign can run around a launch, early access, a demo or a big update, or at any time: a game that is already out is the easiest to measure, and creators can bring players back to one that has gone quiet.

Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release, and the method is the same for a game: pick the right creators, seed across a few niches, read what lands, scale what works. We work with over 60 creators, and the set is different for every game.

We have not put a game campaign on the log yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. If you want to be the first, we will treat it that way.

Tell us the game, the platform and what you want the campaign to do, plus a date if there is one. We'll come back on whether creator content is the right move yet, and what it would look like.
