Game studios

We run TikTok campaigns for game studios and publishers. We hand-pick creators from gaming and meme niches, brief them on the game and what it needs to do, and have them build videos around it. Campaigns are timed to a moment: a launch, early access, a demo, a 1.0 or a big update.

Our last campaign did 10M views across 17 placements. That was a music release, and the method is the same for a game: pick the right creators, seed across a few niches, read what lands, scale what works. We work with over 60 creators, and the set is different for every launch.

We have not put a game campaign on the log yet, and we would rather say that than imply one. If you want to be the first, we will treat it that way.

Tell us the game, the platform, the date and what you want the campaign to do. We'll come back on whether creator content is the right move yet, and what it would look like.
