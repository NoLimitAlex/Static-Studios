How a TikTok game campaign works

FM 1.5 Locked
CH 011.5

Launch

Before, on or after launch day

Creators from gaming and meme niches build videos around the game in their own style, briefed on the game and what each video needs to do.

When
In the weeks before launch, on the day, or any time after it.
How it runs
Smaller tests across a few creator niches first, then more behind the ones that take to the game.
What you get
Views, shares and engagement for every video. Wishlists and sales come from your own Steam or store data.

CH 024.0

Demo / Next Fest

Early access, demos, Next Fest

Creators make videos around a playable build, so the game gets in front of the right players and you see what they respond to before the bigger push.

When
Before or after early access goes live, during a demo or Next Fest, or between early access and 1.0.
Who posts
Gaming and meme creators picked for the game and for where their audience is.

CH 036.5

Update / Season

Updates, seasons, the 1.0

Creator content around a big update, a new season or the 1.0, or months after release to bring the game back up.

Who it is for
Players who drifted off, and new ones too.
What creators post
What is new, shown in their own style of video.

CH 049.0

New market

A country the game has not reached

Creators are picked by where their audience is. Our creators' audiences are biggest in the US and Europe, and a campaign can be aimed at almost any major country.

How
If two creators make the same kind of video, a campaign aimed at that country gets the one whose audience lives there.

CH 0511.5

Fan page

A page about the game, run every month

A TikTok page built around the game, set up and run by us every month: clips, edits and moments on a steady schedule.

Agreed first
How often it posts, how it looks, what footage it can use and how the page presents itself.
Fit
It suits some games better than others, and we say so if yours is not one of them.
