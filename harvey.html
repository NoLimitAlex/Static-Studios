Campaign 001 — Case Study

Dance Until
We're Dead

Harvey
7.4M Views

The Brief

Harvey had a finished track and the problem every independent artist has: a song is only as good as the number of people who actually hear it. Streaming platforms reward records that already have momentum, so the job was to manufacture that first wave of attention — and to do it where music discovery genuinely happens now, which is short-form video.

This was Static Studios' debut campaign, and the goal was simple: put “Dance Until We're Dead” in front of as many genuinely relevant listeners as possible.

The Approach

Rather than pushing the track to one broad, generic audience, we ran it through our creator network across multiple content niches — the difference between a song being seen and a song being saved.

  • Niche matching. The track was placed with creators whose existing audiences already respond to that sound, so it landed as content rather than as an ad.
  • Creator-led UGC. Each creator built the video in their own voice. Native-feeling posts consistently outperform templated promo.
  • Multi-niche spread. Running across several niches at once means the sound isn't dependent on a single corner of the algorithm catching it.
  • Targeted reach. Campaigns can be aimed at a specific demographic or territory, or left to our team to direct.

The Result

The campaign reached 7.4 million views — putting Harvey's track in front of a listenership at a scale that is very difficult to reach unaided, and opening the door to the kind of organic discovery that keeps working after a campaign ends.
